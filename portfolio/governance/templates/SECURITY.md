# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not** open a public issue, pull request or discussion for a security problem.

Report it privately with GitHub's **"Report a vulnerability"** button on this repository's
**Security** tab. We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 days and to agree a disclosure date with you.
These are targets, not guarantees.

Echelon Foundry's coordinated vulnerability disclosure policy, including the good-faith research
statement, is published at
<https://github.com/kemiller2002/echelon-registry/blob/main/portfolio/governance/policies/vulnerability-disclosure-policy.md>.

## What we publish after a fix

Once a fix is available we publish an advisory explaining what failed, why, what was affected, how it
was corrected and what we changed to prevent recurrence. We credit reporters who want to be credited.
